# Account Recovery Policy

## Locked account
An account is locked automatically after 5 failed login attempts. Before unlocking, verify the customer's identity: they must confirm the registered email address or the last four digits of the registered phone number. Once verified, unlock the account and send a password reset link to the registered email. The link expires in 30 minutes.

## Forgot password
Send a password reset link to the registered email address after verifying identity. Never read out or change a password on a call. If the customer no longer has access to the registered email, escalate to the account team for manual verification.

## Login OTP not received
Ask the customer to check the number they are logging in with, wait two minutes and request the OTP only once. Repeated requests can lock the OTP for 30 minutes. If the OTP still does not arrive after 30 minutes, escalate to the account team.

## Profile changes
Email and phone number changes require identity verification first. Account deletion requests must be escalated to the account team, because they need written confirmation.

## Security
Never share order or payment details with a caller who has not been verified. If verification fails twice, stop and offer to escalate.
