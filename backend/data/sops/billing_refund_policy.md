# Billing Refund Policy

## Duplicate charges
If a customer was charged twice for the same order, the second successful transaction is a duplicate and is always eligible for a refund. Before refunding, verify the duplicate: check that two successful transactions exist for the same order, the same amount and the same payment method, within a few minutes of each other. Never refund the original transaction, only the duplicate one.

## Refund approval limits
Refunds of 1000 rupees or less can be executed by the agent directly after verification. Refunds above 1000 rupees need a human approval from the support team before the money is released. While waiting, tell the customer the refund has been sent to the team lead for a quick approval and that they will be told the moment it is done.

## Refund timelines
Once a refund is executed and verified, the money returns to the original payment method in 3 to 5 working days. UPI refunds can take up to 5 working days. Card refunds can take up to 7 working days depending on the bank. Always share the refund reference number with the customer.

## Refund not received
If a customer says a refund has not arrived, first look up the refund reference and its status. If the refund shows completed and it has been less than 5 working days, ask the customer to wait and share the reference number so they can check with their bank. If it has been longer than 7 working days or the status is failed, escalate to the billing team.

## Never promise before verifying
Never tell a customer a refund has been processed until the refund service confirms it. Say it has been submitted, then confirm only after verification.
