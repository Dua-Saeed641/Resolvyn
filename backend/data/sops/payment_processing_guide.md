# Payment Processing Guide

## Payment deducted but order not confirmed
This happens when the payment gateway times out after the bank has already debited the customer. Check the payment transactions for the order. If the transaction status is pending or failed, the amount is automatically reversed to the customer within 3 to 5 working days. Ask the customer to wait and do not place a duplicate order unless they wish to.

## Payment failed or declined
If a payment fails, the customer can retry with another method. UPI failures are usually due to bank server downtime, and card declines are usually due to bank limits or international transaction settings. Ask them to try again after ten minutes or use a different method.

## Invoices and GST
Invoices are generated after dispatch and emailed to the registered email address. GST invoices need the GST number added on the account before the order is placed. Invoices cannot be regenerated with a new GST number after dispatch.

## Subscriptions and plans
Premium plan members get free express delivery and priority support. Plan billing happens on the same date every month. A plan can be cancelled anytime and remains active until the end of the billing period.
