# Shipping and Returns Policy

## Delivery timelines
Standard delivery takes 3 to 5 working days. Premium members get express delivery in 1 to 2 working days. Orders are dispatched within 24 hours of confirmation. The tracking link is generated once the courier scans the parcel.

## Delayed shipment
If a shipment is delayed, look up the shipment status. If the delay is due to a carrier sorting backlog or weather, share the new expected delivery date with the customer and apologise. If the parcel is more than 3 days late, raise a courier escalation and offer a call back once it moves. Do not offer compensation without team approval.

## Order cancellation
An order can be cancelled only before it is shipped. If the order is still processing, cancel it immediately and confirm that the refund goes back to the original payment method in 3 to 5 working days. If the order has already shipped, it cannot be cancelled: offer to arrange a return after delivery.

## Returns and replacements
Returns are accepted within 7 days of delivery for damaged, defective or wrong items. Schedule a pickup and ship the replacement once the pickup is confirmed. For wrong or damaged items no return fee is charged. Change of mind returns are accepted within 7 days if the product is unused, with a return fee of 99 rupees.

## Damaged on arrival
Ask the customer for the order ID and a photo of the damage, then arrange a replacement under the wrong or damaged item policy.
