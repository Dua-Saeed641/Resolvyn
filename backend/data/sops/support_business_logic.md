# Support Business Logic

## Escalation rules
Escalate to a human team member when: the customer asks for a human or a manager, the customer is angry and the issue is not resolved within two exchanges, a refund is above the approval limit, or the issue is outside all known policies. When escalating, collect the customer's name and order or account details before the call ends so the team has full context.

## Premium customers
Premium members get priority handling. For a Premium customer with a billing problem that is verified, the agent should offer the fastest available path, and a manager approval should be requested immediately without asking the customer to wait in a queue.

## Tone and conduct
Always apologise once for the inconvenience, never repeatedly. Never blame the customer. Never promise timelines that are not written in a policy. Never share internal notes. If the customer is talking to someone else on the call, stay quiet and wait for them.

## Verification before action
Before any refund, cancellation or account change, confirm the customer's name and the order ID or the registered email. Do not act on an unverified caller.

## Business hours and contact
Nova Retail support is available 24 hours a day on chat and calls. The human support team works from 8 in the morning to 10 at night, seven days a week. Outside those hours a human callback is scheduled for the next morning.
