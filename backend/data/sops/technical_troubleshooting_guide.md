# Technical Troubleshooting Guide

## Bluetooth headphones will not pair
Factory reset the headphones by holding the power button and the volume down button together for ten seconds until the light blinks white twice. Remove the headphones from the phone's Bluetooth list, then pair again from scratch. Keep the headphones within one metre of the phone while pairing.

## Mobile app crashes or freezes
Clear the app storage and cache, then uninstall and reinstall the latest version from the store and sign in again. Make sure the phone has at least 500 MB of free space. If the crash continues after reinstalling, collect the phone model and app version and escalate to the technical team.

## Device will not charge
Try a different cable and adapter, and clean the charging port gently with a dry brush. Charge for at least thirty minutes before turning it on. If the charging light does not come on at all, the device may need a replacement under warranty.

## Smart kettle not heating
Make sure the kettle is properly seated on its base and that there is enough water above the minimum level. Unplug it for five minutes to reset the thermal cutoff, then try again.

## Warranty
All electronics carry a one year warranty against manufacturing defects. Warranty replacement needs the order ID and a description of the fault. Physical or water damage is not covered.
